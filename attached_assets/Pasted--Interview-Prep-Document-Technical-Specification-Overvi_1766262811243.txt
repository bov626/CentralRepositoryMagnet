# Interview Prep Document - Technical Specification

## Overview
Build a structured interview prep document template that displays company research, interviewer intel, and custom questions for each scheduled interview.

## Document Structure

### Header Section
```
Company: [Company Name]
Role: [Job Title]
Interview Date/Time: [DateTime - formatted as "Monday, January 15, 2025 at 2:00 PM EST"]
Interview Format: [Phone Screen / Video / Panel / In-Person]
Interviewer(s): [Names + Titles if known, otherwise "TBD"]
Job Description: [Clickable link to original JD]
```

### Section 1: Company Overview
**Header:** Company Overview

**Content:**
- What they do (plain English description, 2-3 sentences)
- Company size (number of employees, revenue if public)
- Recent funding/growth trajectory
- Company culture vibe (based on Glassdoor, LinkedIn, reviews)

**Format:** Bullet points, clean and scannable

---

### Section 2: Recent News & Updates
**Header:** Recent News & Updates

**Content:**
- Last 90 days: Product launches, acquisitions, layoffs, leadership changes
- Earnings call highlights (if applicable for public companies)
- Any pain points or challenges the company is facing

**Format:** Bullet points with dates where relevant

---

### Section 3: Role Breakdown
**Header:** What This Role Actually Does

**Content:**
- Day-to-day responsibilities (practical translation of JD)
- Team structure (who you'd be working with)
- What success looks like in first 90 days
- Key skills they're actually looking for (beyond what JD lists)

**Format:** Bullet points

---

### Section 4: Your Interviewer(s)
**Header:** About Your Interviewer(s)

**Content (if interviewer known):**
- Name and title
- Background and tenure at company
- Previous roles/experience
- LinkedIn activity highlights (what they post/care about)
- Personal interests/hobbies (scraped from social profiles)
- **Rapport builders:** 2-3 conversation hooks based on the above

**Content (if interviewer unknown):**
- Display: "Interviewer details not yet available"

**Format:** Short paragraphs or bullet points per interviewer

---

### Section 5: Tailored Questions You Should Ask
**Header:** Questions That Show You Did Your Homework

**Content:**
- 3-5 custom questions built from company research
- Each question should reference specific company news, challenges, or initiatives

**Example format:**
```
- "I saw you just launched [product/feature]—how is that changing the roadmap for this role?"
- "Your Q3 earnings mentioned [challenge]—is this team directly tasked with solving that?"
- "I noticed [interviewer name] previously worked on [project]—will I be collaborating with that team?"
```

**Format:** Bullet points, questions in quotes

---

### Section 6: Wilson's Universal Interview Questions
**Header:** Universal Questions That Work Anywhere

**Content:**
Standard set of questions that work in any interview:
- What does success look like in this role after 6 months?
- What's the biggest challenge the team is facing right now?
- How does this role contribute to the company's top priorities this year?
- What do people who succeed here have in common?
- What's your favorite part about working here?

**Format:** Bullet points, questions in regular text

---

### Footer Section
**Content:**
"We'll send a tailored thank-you email on your behalf after the interview for follow-up."

**Format:** Italicized text, centered or left-aligned at bottom

---

## Technical Requirements

### Display Format
- Clean, professional layout
- Mobile-responsive (clients will view on phones before interviews)
- Scannable headers with clear visual hierarchy
- Maximum 2-3 pages when printed
- Each section clearly separated (use dividers or spacing)

### Data Structure
Each prep document should be tied to:
- Interview ID
- Client ID
- Job application ID (links to "Applied Jobs" panel)
- Company name
- Role title
- Interview datetime
- Job description URL

### User Interaction
- Accessible from calendar view in "Interviews" tab
- Click on calendar event → opens full prep document
- Should be viewable/downloadable as PDF
- Mobile-optimized view

### Admin (Wilson's) Input Fields
For each prep document, Wilson needs to input:
- Company overview details
- Recent news (3-5 items)
- Role breakdown
- Interviewer details (if known)
- 3-5 tailored questions
- Any additional notes

### Default Content
- "Wilson's Universal Interview Questions" section is static (same for all docs)
- Footer message is static
- Header fields auto-populate from interview/job data

---

## UI/UX Notes
- Use clear visual hierarchy (headers, subheaders, body text)
- Consider color-coding sections for quick scanning
- White space is important—don't cram content
- Bold key information (company name, interviewer names, dates)
- Make job description link clearly clickable/tappable

## File Format
- Primary: Web view (HTML/CSS)
- Secondary: PDF export option
- Both should maintain formatting and readability
